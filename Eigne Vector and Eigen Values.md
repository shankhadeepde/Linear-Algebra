
> **Definitions**

1. Left Eigen Vector: $x^TA = \lambda x^T$
2. Right Eigen Vector: $Ax = \lambda x$

> **Theorems**

1. $\lambda_1 \ and \ \lambda_2$ are left and right eigen values of matrix $\boldsymbol{A}$ and if $\lambda_1 \neq \lambda_2$, then $x_1 \perp x_2$ where $x_1 \ and \ x_2$ are left and right eigen vectors corresponding to $\lambda_1 \ and \ \lambda_2$
2. if $\lambda$ is an non-zero eigen value of $\boldsymbol{AB}$ then it's also an eigen value of $\boldsymbol{BA}$
